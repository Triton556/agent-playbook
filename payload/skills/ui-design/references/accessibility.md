# Accessible interaction checks

Apply to the changed user path, not as a second full audit. Follow the project's accessibility requirements and relevant [WCAG guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/).

- Controls have visible names or accessible labels; icons do not rely on shape alone to communicate unfamiliar actions. Required fields are identified before submission.
- Keyboard users can reach and operate controls; focus remains visible and moves predictably after dialogs/errors. Drag-only actions have a keyboard/control alternative.
- Errors identify the affected field and a recovery action. Preserve valid inputs and user filters when retrying. Announce important committed results, not every animation frame.
- A pending action gives visible feedback, prevents unintended duplicate submission and reaches success or an actionable error. Preserve input on recoverable failure.
- Text and meaningful graphics remain readable at the intended size; color is not the only signal. Verify contrast against the applicable project standard.
- Respect reduced-motion settings; offer pause for continuous motion where needed. Motion must not delay access to content or hide state.

A screenshot verifies appearance, not keyboard behavior or assistive-technology support. Report unperformed checks explicitly; do not claim certification from this checklist.
