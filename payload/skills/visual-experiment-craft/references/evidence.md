## Targeted current source observations

- Cube Graph: worker-based bounded search, validation of returned path, instanced scene objects, camera fit accounting for UI, multi-pass rendering.
- Token Town: crowd uses a nonlinear token transform, live/snapshot status, separate counter easing, instance reuse, distance-limited trail work and lower-resolution effects.
- River Bend: rendering and model update stages, camera offsets and adaptive quality; late render-stat reset warrants care when counting earlier passes.
- Prompt: generated step timeline and approximate timing/energy calculations, multiple semantic scene levels, camera adaptation.
- Benchmark Mountain: instanced scenery, camera targeting and view offsets, multi-pass rendering and quality adjustment.
- Earthquake Tower: pixel-count limit, viewport-dependent view offsets, main-loop and effect quality stages.

The source files were read in temporary storage; their SHA-256 receipts and targeted-read scope live in the research report outside this skill. Minified libraries were not audited. Existing historical optics source claims were retained as historical evidence rather than re-certified.

## Corrections that matter for transfer

Initial easing and autoplay can change input and numbers during observation. Pause and ownership must be deliberate. A metric can remain stale while playback is paused. Mobile adaptation can still clip objects or allow overlays to cover controls. Light-background glass can lose contrast. A displayed FPS label cannot establish complete render throughput. These are reasons to validate the new implementation, not universal claims that every reference fails.
