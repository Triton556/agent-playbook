# Major material boundaries before and after voxel construction

For natural markings or prominent material/color regions, inspect their large contour independently of the object's silhouette, lighting and cross-view consistency. Name the photographic basis or explicit authored stylization; a breed's allowed color category does not specify the shape of its patches.

During 2D design review inspect left/right, underside and relevant oblique views at common scale and anatomical landmarks. During 3D review inspect material-ID/binary masks without shadows, both native cells and an overview where individual one/two-cell steps recede. Record long axial runs and their major turns in cells, world length and relative body size. These are diagnostic observations, not a universal numeric fail threshold. Preserve intentional pixel steps and supported stylization.

Issue two separate findings: the source material contour is justified, and 3D preserves the accepted contour. Consistent transfer of an unreviewed rectangle does not establish a good contour. Adding small noise around the same rectangle does not repair its major shape. Bind pass/fail/unverified to the current revision and inspected region; unavailable views remain unverified.
