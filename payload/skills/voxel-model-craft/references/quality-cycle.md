# Function, scale and evidence after Gen2 feedback

Read the general [artifact quality cycle](../../product-engineering/references/quality-cycle.md) for repeated defects or a batch. Keep that workflow general; the following are voxel adapters and scoped examples.

- Before new detailed voxel construction or a changed silhouette/action, run the [orthographic design preflight](design-preflight.md). Start with the object/kit passport, inspect six consistent 2D views and the applicable common-scale kit comparison, repair the plan, and retain its revision for later voxel-to-plan review. Treat style, context, readability and intended use as explicit current criteria at both stages.
- Declare world size, master voxel pitch, coordinate origin/normalization and intended display scale separately. A long fox and deer cannot be judged from equal-height thumbnails. Use a common pixels-per-meter side sheet for proportions; auto-fit is appropriate for inspecting one shape and must be labelled.
- Name the functional face and its feature: face/eyes, headlights/windshield, passenger door, book spines, controls, latch or useful cavity. Render four distinct directions when occlusion matters; a before/after pair at one angle remains one direction. Zoom must retain full-grid occluders, not crop geometry to expose a hidden eye. Inspect creature heads front and both profiles, and ambiguous hand grips at their real review size.
- An eye gate applies when visible eyes belong to the intended design. A deliberately eyeless creature has an explicit style exception. A flat instrument's1.05m length is not its Z thickness; declare the primary dimension/orientation before deriving pitch and common-scale captions.
- Capture part ownership before union. Test forbidden overlap independently of colors. Axle/hub and wing-root contacts are required attachments; free wheel/membrane clearance is separate. A contact check does not know that a hand is holding the bow frame rather than its string: use typed subparts and inspect the action.
- A static opaque render master may omit hidden core cells. Preserve its exterior colors/geometry, intended load cores and connected editable structure. Restore minimum original-cell bridges when hollowing separates branches. Export the declared opaque exterior source so artificial inaccessible inner faces do not bloat the mesh. A filled collision/volume model uses its own representation. No universal shell threshold or FPS claim follows from fewer cells.
- Choose wear from cause and material: damp bases, staggered irregular masonry, edge chips, contact abrasion, seams along wood grain and gravity/deposition of snow. Maintain deliberate quiet surfaces; three tones is a useful local choice, not a universal gate. Major missing joints/cracks have geometry; small pigment wear may remain palette-only.
- Scenes declare land/water/path/dock/boat substrates, object contact footprints, epoch/biome and a life anchor. A bridge can cross water; an ordinary dirt path cannot. A desert oven needs a plausible bread-making story, rather than an automatic ban. Ground infill follows actual feet/roots instead of stamping a rectangular bounding-box podium.

## Versioned original examples

`python scripts/gen21_generate.py --model oak --seed 153 --output <directory>` produces original Gen2.1 VOX/OBJ/MTL plus a construction manifest. Use `--category` or omit selectors for all40. Earlier Gen1 and Gen2 helpers remain available. These recipes demonstrate repairs, not general-purpose reconstruction of any reference. OBJ uses voxel-cell units; apply the declared pitch for a scene in metres.

Use `scripts/semantic_contract.py` for declared ray visibility, allowed contacts, forbidden overlap, clearance, material depth and substrate coverage. A missing mask returns unknown. These static checks do not judge recognition, biological correctness, pose naturalness or beauty. Pair them with inspected current images and the general revision-aware quality ledger.

Pilot evidence: human Gen2 feedback; saved GLM audit reconciled against actual grids; independent bad/good fixtures; four-view and head/hand inspection. The [official Toyota NNP10 archive](https://www.toyota-global.com/company/history_of_toyota/75years/vehicle_lineage/car/id60012578/) and its front/side photograph supply compact-van proportions; other world sizes are authored stylized targets. Existing source provenance/license boundaries remain in [Evidence](evidence.md).

The general [perception protocol](../../product-engineering/references/human-perspective-review.md) governs observation, intent and cause hygiene. Keep voxel-specific view, scale, eye/grip/contact and palette checks here. A perceptual exemplar can retain an unknown cause; a reusable technical rule needs a verified mechanism. A repeated surface detail is grouped by coverage before claiming a common generator cause.
